        Quick start
        
      
          
        
        1. Power the travel router, then Node 1 and the TurtleBot. Both nodes start automatically (systemd).
          2. 
        
        2. Check the pills at the top: Node 1, Node 2 and Link must be green.
          3. 
        
        3. Driver sits 45–55 cm from the C270 and presses Calibrate (or button A on the cockpit) — eyes open for 5 s.
          4. 
        
        4. Place the robot on the lane. The readiness list must be empty, then press START.
          5. 
        
        5. For a scored trial open Trials, pick the scenario, press Start recording, run it, press Stop & score.
          6. 
        
        6. After CONTROLLED_STOP the driver must be alert for 1.5 s; then press RESET (or cockpit button B) and START.
          7. 
      
        
    
        
    
        
      
        States
        
      
        
        
        STANDBY
        Armed, not moving. Power-up state and after RESET.
        
        
        NORMAL CRUISE
        Driver alert. 0.16 m/s with lane keeping.
        
        
        WARNING
        Eyes closed ≥ 1.2 s, a yawn, high PERCLOS or looking away ≥ 2 s. Intermittent beep.
        
        
        SPEED REDUCTION
        Closure ≥ 2.5 s, 3 yawns/min or the face is not visible ≥ 0.5 s. 50 % speed.
        
        
        CONTROLLED STOP
        Closure ≥ 4 s or driver missing ≥ 5 s. Ramps to 0 at 0.06 m/s² while staying in lane. Latched.
        
        
        EMERGENCY STOP
        Obstacle < 20 cm, heartbeat lost > 150 ms, lane lost > 0.3 s, lidar fault or E-STOP. Immediate stop.
